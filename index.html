Section 1: Match Flow & Timing

  • Match Length: Standard match consists of four 2-minute quarters.
  • Alternating Play: Players take turns releasing one ball at a time (Player A, then Player B).
  • The Supply Pause: When the launch rack is completely empty, the match clock strictly pauses.
  • Retrieval Phase: Players collect all balls from the scoring arena and return them to the launch station before the clock resumes.

Section 2: The "Redo" Fault Rule

  • Rail Derailment: If the ball falls off the railway track at any point before making clean contact with the wall, the play is ruled a Fault.
  • The Redo: A Fault results in an immediate redo. The player retrieves that specific ball, returns to the starting line, and rolls again with no penalty or point recorded.

Section 3: The Scoring Matrix

Target Zone Points Description
🎯 Small Stone 3 Points Highest difficulty target zone.

Section 4: Overtime Rules (Tie-Breaker)

  • Overtime Trigger: If total score is tied at the end of the 4th quarter, the match enters Overtime.
  • The Format: Overtime is played in a no-clock sudden death one-roll format.
